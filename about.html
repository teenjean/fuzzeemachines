hey!

fuzzee machines here again. As you probably already know, my work surrounds the ever-evolving relationships between craft and computation, fiber and electronics, programmable soft/bio machines, DIY computing, synthesizers, data and poetry - I could go on. So, what is this space? Yet another outlet to showcase more work? Hmmm - yes and no.

As the days have rolled by, I've been wondering: At world's end, what would we preserve? What would I preserve? Given the current state of the world, preservation of histories, curiosity, artifacts has, I think, become increasingly important. Not to mention the spaces on the web that are still here, but long forgotten e.g. MySpace, Limewire, "ugly" websites. Or are these things meant to "die" an undying death. When Arlo Iron Cloud, a broadcaster for KILI Radio, the voice of the Lakota Nation on the Pine Ridge Reservation, was posed the question, "What would you preserve to restart the United States following a nuclear apocalypse?", he responded he might burn the Constitution and the Bill of Rights. He argued true democratic ideals survive dynamically through oral storytelling and community connection.

There is a beauty in burning. Just like there is a beauty in keeping. Now, I don't think this web zine will be THE ZINE to impact us all. No. I just care about things. If I can share what I know (very little) and potentially transmit that passion and foolishness to others all while embracing the successes and shit-eating fails that come with fabricating stuff, maybe I've done my part.

Welcome to the web zine and archive "if whatever happens is meant to happen then whatever".
